# Character Bible v0.1

This folder defines the fixed visual identity of the creator's internet avatar.

## Role

The character is the creator's **internet spokesperson / avatar**.

It can observe the internet and the physical world and present the creator's ideas in a recognizable way.

It does **not** independently invent major positions for the creator.

> Creator = judgment  
> Character = expression

## Non-negotiable silhouette

The character must remain recognizable from a small thumbnail.

### Head
- simple white circle
- black rough outline
- no realistic facial features
- usually blank or nearly blank
- expression comes mostly from pose

### Hat
- bright blue triangle
- loose hand-drawn stroke
- slightly oversized compared with head
- always contains one orange star

### Body
- black stick / brush body
- very simple limbs
- no detailed anatomy
- no permanent clothes unless a specific story temporarily requires a costume

### Color anchors

| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Main blue | `#149BFF` |
| Star orange | `#FF6A00` |
| Ink black | `#111111` |
| Paper | `#F7F4EC` |
| Neutral gray | `#8B8B8B` |

Exact output colors may shift slightly with hand-drawn texture, but blue + orange must remain visually dominant.

## Proportions

Use these as guidance, not geometric law:
- head diameter ≈ 1 body-width unit
- hat width ≈ 1.35–1.55 × head diameter
- total body height below head ≈ 2.0–2.5 × head diameter
- limbs remain thin and gestural

Avoid cute chibi deformation that makes the body tiny and head huge.

## Expression vocabulary

Prefer expression through pose.

Recommended reusable poses:
- neutral standing
- thinking: one hand near chin
- pointing: one raised index / hand
- surprised: slight lean + two motion marks
- wave
- thumbs-up / small celebration

Avoid detailed eyes, mouths, eyebrows unless the specific generation needs a tiny temporary mark.

## Personality visible in pose

The character should feel:
- curious
- slightly odd
- restrained
- observant

It should rarely look hyperactive or overly cute.

## Relationship to secondary characters

Secondary characters may exist, but they must not visually overpower the avatar.

For AI/tool stories, a small white helper robot is acceptable:
- round white body
- black screen face
- blue accent
- visually smaller or equal in weight to protagonist

The robot is not part of the permanent core identity.

## Do not drift into

- anime protagonist
- corporate mascot
- 3D toy
- detailed wizard
- superhero
- realistic human
- sheep / animal character
- black-robed historical official
- heavily costumed IP figure

The point is recognizability through **extreme simplicity**.
